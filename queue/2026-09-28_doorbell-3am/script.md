# Doorbell, 3:12 AM — 30s jump scare

**Format:** jump scare · **Length:** short, 30s · **Goal:** 3s retention + follows per 1K
**Premise:** A doorbell camera logs four motion alerts in two minutes. Alerts 1–3 show a
pale figure getting closer each time. Alert 4 shows an empty porch, then the figure's
face fills the lens from below.
**The image people remember:** fisheye night-vision face, too close, eyes reflecting IR.

## Hook (0–2s)
- Visual: doorbell-cam frame, IR monochrome, empty porch, app UI chrome, timestamp 3:12:04 AM
- On-screen text: **4 motion alerts. 3:12 AM. Nobody there.**
- Sound: phone notification buzz, then porch silence (crickets, one moth hit)
- Rubric: curiosity 5 · visual stop 4 · specificity 5 · promise fits 5 · no telegraph 5 = 24/25

## Ending
Cut to black on the door-opened notification. No music sting, no logo. The silence is the ending.
