# The Basement — 70s found footage

**Format:** found footage · **Length:** long, 70s (Creator Rewards) · **Goal:** full watch + follows per 1K
**Premise:** The last video on a phone left behind in a rental house. The tenant goes to
the basement at 1:47 AM to find a knocking sound. There is a small chained door at floor
level. He knocks three times. It knocks four. The lock is open. Something comes out and
goes upstairs. The basement door locks from the kitchen side.
**The image people remember:** the small door swinging open and a long pale arm reaching for the phone.

## Hook (0–3s)
- Visual: vertical phone footage, phone flashlight, top of a basement staircase, door frame, darkness below. Timestamp overlay 1:47 AM.
- On-screen text: **Last video on a phone left in a rental. 1:47 AM.**
- Sound: three slow knocks from below, muffled, then the tenant's breath.
- Rubric: curiosity 5 · visual stop 4 · specificity 5 · promise fits 5 · no telegraph 5 = 24/25

## Ending
Black card with the last line. Hold 4s. No music. Optional final frame: the listing-style
text "3 bed · 2 bath · finished basement" for half a second if the owner wants a dark joke.

## SFX list
knocks (3, 3, 3, 4 — different rooms, keep them distinct) · stairs creak · breath (steady → fast) · furnace tick · drip · dust patter · chain tinkle · wood creak · door slam · wet slide · phone clatter on concrete · dragging + wet breathing · slow heavy steps on wood · upstairs door + deadbolt

## Rules kept
- Length 70s ≥ 65s floor. Scare inside 55–75%. Hook text never mentions a scare.
- No blood on the arm, no wound, no face on the thing, no minors, no real address.
