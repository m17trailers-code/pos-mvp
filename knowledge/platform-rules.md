# TikTok platform rules that affect M17

Last verified: 2026-09-25. Re-check monthly and after any TikTok policy email.
Sources: TikTok Creator Academy (eligibility), TikTok Shop Seller University
(affiliate pilot program, Policy Pulse May 2026), TikTok Community Guidelines
(update effective 2026-09-24).

## Creator Rewards Program (goal 1)
- 10,000+ followers.
- 100,000+ authentic video views in the last 30 days.
- Personal account, 18+, in an eligible country (US is eligible).
- Only original videos **over 60 seconds** earn. Duets, stitches, photo posts,
  ads and sponsored content do not. We use a **65-second floor** for margin.
- Reported RPM roughly $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views; treat as a range,
  not a promise.

## TikTok Shop affiliate (goal 2)
- Application currently under appeal. Until approved, no shoppable posts.
- Creators under 5,000 followers enter the **Affiliate Creator Pilot Program**:
  max **3 shoppable videos per day** and **3 shoppable LIVEs per week**.
  Graduation needs 5,000 followers, Creator Health Rating above 176 and about
  10 organic orders.
- Accounts tagged "Extended Pilot Creator" for quality issues drop to
  3 shoppable videos **per week** and 1 LIVE per week.
- Platform-wide cap from 2026-05-11: 30 shoppable videos and 60 shoppable
  photo posts per day. Not our constraint yet.
- **Current limit we plan against: 3 shoppable videos/day** (mirrored in
  `channels/m17.yaml` → `limits`). Update both when status changes.

## Disclosure
- Any affiliate or brand mention gets `#ad` or "commission earned" in the
  caption. Also toggle TikTok's own branded-content / promotional label when
  posting. FTC rules apply to US creators.

## Community Guidelines (update effective 2026-09-24)
- Three enforcement levels: removal, 18+ age restriction, or ineligible for
  the For You feed. Horror that is "extremely gory, disturbing or violent" gets
  removed; realistic graphic imagery can be age-gated or de-recommended, which
  kills reach even without a strike.
- Our lines (stricter than TikTok's): no real gore, no blood pooling or wounds
  in close-up, no self-harm imagery or implication, no minors in danger, no
  real people's likeness, no copyrighted characters or film footage.
- Fiction should read as fiction: "found footage" framing is fine, but never
  claim a real event, real missing person or real location incident.
- No misleading edits presented as real news; no dangerous-challenge framing.

## Account health after the automation flag
- All actions on TikTok are by the owner's hand, in the app, at human pace.
- No third-party schedulers through the owner's session; no mass follow,
  mass like, or copy-paste comment bursts.
- Post from the same device and network the owner normally uses.
- Optional later: the official TikTok Content Posting API. Not built now.
