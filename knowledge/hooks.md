## Hook scoring rubric (used by /hooks and /review)
Score 1–5 on each:
- Curiosity gap: does the viewer need to know what happens?
- Visual stop: is the first frame different from a normal feed?
- Specificity: a place, a time, an object, not "something scary"
- Promise fits runtime: a 70s video needs a bigger question than a 25s one
- No telegraphing: text never says "wait for it" / "jump scare"
